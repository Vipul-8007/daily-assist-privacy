Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 18 November 2026

Our Privacy Approach

Daily Assist Version 1.0 is designed as an offline-first application. Personal information and records entered into the app are intended to remain on your device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily Assist. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the Daily Assist Android application handles information when you use the application.

Version 1.0 is an offline-first personal productivity and record-management application.

2. Information Stored in the Application

Depending on the features you use, you may enter the following information into Daily Assist:

  • Tasks and Agendas: Task titles, categories, priorities, due dates, reminders, and completion status.
  • Personal Finance: Income, expenses, categories, bills, loans, and lending or borrowing records (Udhaari).
  • Notes and Shopping: Personal notes, shopping lists, item names, quantities, and checklist information.
  • Documents and Warranties: Document names, tags, expiry dates, retailer information, and receipt images or attachments.
  • App Preferences: Language, theme, application preferences, and legal acceptance records.

This information is entered by you and is used to provide the application's features.

3. Local Data Storage

Daily Assist Version 1.0 stores application records in a local SQLite database on your Android device.

  • Database name: DailyLifeAssistant.db
  • Data is stored in the application's private storage area.
  • Application records are not uploaded to a Daily Assist cloud server.
  • Other applications cannot normally access the app's private storage.

4. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored in Daily Assist is used to provide application functionality, including:

  • Displaying your saved records.
  • Managing tasks, notes, and shopping lists.
  • Displaying income and expense summaries.
  • Showing financial categories and reports.
  • Managing loan and payment records.
  • Providing local reminders and notifications.

5. Internet and Network Usage

Daily Assist Version 1.0 is designed to work offline. The application does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

The application does not send your personal application records to a Daily Assist cloud server in this release.

6. Third-Party Services

Daily Assist Version 1.0 does not intentionally use third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud services to collect or transmit your personal application records.

7. Advertising and Tracking

Daily Assist Version 1.0 does not display advertisements and does not intentionally use advertising SDKs or behavioural tracking technologies.

8. Documents and Attachments

Documents, receipt images, and other attachments added through supported application features are stored locally in the application's storage area.

These attachments are not uploaded to a Daily Assist cloud server in Version 1.0.

9. Permissions

Daily Assist may request permissions required for specific application features.

  • Notifications: Used to display local reminders and notifications.
  • Media or File Access: Used when you select images or files for supported document and attachment features.

Permissions are requested only when required by the relevant application functionality. Actual permissions depend on your Android version and the features included in the installed release.

10. Data Sharing

Daily Assist does not sell, rent, or trade your personal application records.

Version 1.0 does not intentionally transfer your personal application records to third parties or a Daily Assist cloud server.

11. Data Retention

Your application records remain stored locally until you delete them, clear the application's storage, or uninstall Daily Assist.

12. Data Deletion

You can delete individual records using the relevant features available inside the application.

You can also clear application data through Android:

Android Settings → Apps → Daily Assist → Storage → Clear Storage

Uninstalling the application removes its private local application data, subject to Android device behaviour and any copies you have separately created.

13. Children's Privacy

Daily Assist is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application does not intentionally collect children's personal information through an online service.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application functionality or privacy practices change.

Updated versions will be published at this Privacy Policy URL. Material changes may also be presented through the application's legal information screen.
